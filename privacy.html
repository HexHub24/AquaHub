Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version

We collect the minimum data needed to run the app. We don't sell your data. We don't track you across the web. We don't show ads. Your tank data is yours.

What we collect

  • Account info: email and password (the password is hashed; we never see the plain text).
  • Your tank data: tanks, livestock, water tests, notes — anything you enter into the app.
  • Basic logs: when you sign in, basic error reports, to keep the app running.

What we don't collect

  • We don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't sell your email or any other data to third parties.
  • We don't show ads, so we have no advertising trackers.

How we store your data

Your data is stored on Supabase, a hosted Postgres provider. Connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Row Level Security ensures only you can read your own tanks.

Your rights

You can export your data at any time from your account settings. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Email us if you have any questions.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use any analytics or advertising cookies.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at hello@aquahub.app.

This policy is intentionally simple. If you need a more formal privacy notice for a specific legal purpose, contact us.
