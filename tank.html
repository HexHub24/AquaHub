Bioload breakdown by species

    Scores are based on adult body mass (length²·⁵), with diet, activity, and taxonomy adjustments — not the "1 inch per gallon" rule. 1 unit = one adult neon tetra (1.5"). Larger or messier species scale up sharply: a 6" angelfish ≈ 58 units, a 5" pleco ≈ 17 units. Plant offset is capped at 40% of total load.
